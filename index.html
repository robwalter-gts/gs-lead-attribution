Lead Source Field Model

How Source Category, Lead Source, and Lead Source Detail relate to each other, how a UTM-tagged click turns into those field values, and how the old Salesforce source values map into it.

Prepared byRob Walter, Ground Team Solutions
StatusProposed — not yet built
Updated2026‑09‑10

The framework has three levels. Source Category is the reporting rollup. Lead Source is the specific channel — a 17‑value closed list, shown below grouped under its category. Lead Source Detail is the free‑text layer underneath each one; Figure 3 shows how it gets filled in without turning into another list to maintain. Figure 2 shows the piece in between: how a UTM-tagged click becomes those field values in the first place.

For each of those 17 values, Lead Source Detail stays a text field rather than a picklist — but most of it never gets hand-typed. Where a clean value already exists somewhere in HubSpot, a workflow copies it in. Only the sources with nothing upstream to pull from get typed by hand, and five sources still need this mapping worked out.

Still open: what goes in Lead Source Detail?

For the five System-Populated, form-driven sources above (Website, Paid Search, Paid Social, Webinars, Display), a few options are on the table: the name of the form someone converted on, the utm_campaign value when one is present, or both — with a separate Most Recent Campaign field so neither gets lost. Figure 3 currently shows the form-name option; nothing is decided yet.

This is a proposed model, not yet built. Still open: which HubSpot portal to build in, what Lead Source Detail should hold for form-driven sources (flagged above), and the five Lead Source values that still need a Detail mapping worked out (dashed, in Figure 3).
