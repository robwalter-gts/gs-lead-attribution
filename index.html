Lead Source Field Model

A proposed design for how leads get a Lead Source and Lead Source Detail, how that reaches sales, and how it connects to campaigns.

Prepared byRob Walter, Ground Team Solutions
StatusProposed — not built
Updated2026‑10‑14

The model has two fields. Lead Source is the specific channel, chosen from a closed list. Lead Source Detail is a proposed picklist that adds one more level of detail, so reports can filter by picking a value instead of matching exact text. The earlier Category level is removed. Campaign stays separate from both. Everything on this page is a proposal. Amber dashed items are open or not built.

Lead Source

Lead Source is now a standalone list. Without Category, some values lose the grouping that gave them part of their meaning, so they are flagged for review. No replacement values are proposed.

  • ATradeshow, Conference. The Detail example is “Trade Show”, with the specific event identified by campaign. That overlaps with these two Lead Source values. Decide which level says “trade show”.
  • BDisplay, Organic List, Pay-Per-Lead, Purchased List, Webinars. These sat under Paid Media or Organic, which supplied part of their meaning. Check that each name still stands on its own, for example whether Webinars means paid or organic.
  • COther. Kept as the catch-all in the old-value mapping below, but it was never part of the 17-value list. Confirm it is a Lead Source value.

Two ways a lead enters

UTM values only exist on web conversions. Imported or offline leads, such as a trade-show list, have no UTMs, so their Lead Source is set by hand and the specific event is identified by campaign membership.

Lead Source Detail as a picklist

Detail is proposed as a picklist so reports can filter by choosing a value, with no exact-text matching. The picklist choices, and the rule that picks one for each source, are not defined yet.

Still open: what fills Detail for form-driven sources

For Website, Paid Search, Paid Social, Webinars and Display, the possibilities include:

  • the name of the form the lead converted on,
  • the utm_campaign value when one is present,
  • or both.

A picklist also needs a fixed set of choices, so each possibility has to map onto that set. Who adds new choices as forms, vendors, and events appear is part of the same decision. Nothing is decided yet.

First touch and last touch

Phase 1 records the first touch only. Lead Source and Detail are set at the first conversion. Most-recent, or last-touch, attribution is deferred. It is not designed or built, and the diagram marks it only to show where phase 1 stops.

Lead source is not revenue influence

This model answers where a lead came from. It does not connect a campaign to revenue, and no campaign-to-revenue path is drawn because none exists yet.

Lead Source Detail

  • What fills Detail for form-driven sources: form name, utm_campaign, or both.
  • Whether Content Type stays as its own field next to Detail.

Mapping and records

  • The UTM-to-Lead Source mapping table is not drafted, and utm_audience has no HubSpot property yet.
  • The review flags on the Lead Source list, and the five sources with no Detail path yet.
  • Which attribution fields appear on lead and contact records, and how they sync.
  • The intended 1:1 match between HubSpot and Salesforce campaigns is not verified or built.

Build and reporting

  • Where to build: which HubSpot portal, and whether Salesforce changes the existing Lead Source picklist or adds a new field.
  • PunchOut orders have no Lead Source value, and their attribution is phase 2.
  • The influence window for order correlation.
