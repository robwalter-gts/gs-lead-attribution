Lead Source Field Model

A proposed design for how leads get a Lead Source and Lead Source Detail, how that reaches sales, and how it connects to campaigns.

Prepared byRob Walter, Ground Team Solutions
StatusProposed, not built. Lead Source list awaiting sign-off
Updated2026‑10‑14

The model has two fields. Lead Source is the specific channel, chosen from a closed list. Lead Source Detail is a picklist, a decided part of the model, that adds one more level of detail, so reports can filter by picking a value instead of matching exact text. The earlier Category level is removed. Campaign stays separate from both. Everything on this page is a proposal. Amber dashed items are open or not built.

Two ways a lead enters

UTM values only exist on web conversions. Imported or offline leads, such as a trade-show list, have no UTMs, so their Lead Source is set by hand and the specific event is identified by campaign membership.

First-touch and most-recent UTM values

Each conversion that carries UTM values can be recorded twice: once to keep the first touch, and once to show the latest touch. The two sets sit in separate fields, so the latest touch never overwrites the first.

First-touch UTM values

What brought the lead in the first time?

  • Written: at the first conversion that carries UTM values, then kept.
  • Used for: Lead Source, through the lookup below, and Detail if UTM values are chosen to fill it.
  • Applies to: web conversions only. Imported leads have no UTM values.

Most-recent UTM values

What brought the lead back most recently?

  • Proposed: new fields, one for each UTM value. Names to be agreed. Not built.
  • Written: at the first conversion, then overwritten at every later conversion that carries UTM values, so they always hold the latest touch.
  • Used for: reporting on the latest touch. They do not change Lead Source or Detail.
  • Proposed rule: if there are no UTM values on the latest conversion, the fields keep their last values.

Lead Source Detail as a picklist

Detail is a picklist, so reports can filter by choosing a value, with no exact-text matching. The choices are suggested below for approval. The rule that picks one for web sources depends on the open decision further down. Who owns the list is not decided.

Still open: what fills Detail for form-driven sources

For Website, Paid Search, Paid Social, Webinars and Paid Display, the possibilities include:

  • the HubSpot First Conversion value, which is the name of the form the lead first converted on,
  • the utm_campaign value when one is present,
  • or both.

A picklist also needs a fixed set of choices, so each possibility has to map onto that set. Who adds new choices as forms, vendors, and events appear is part of the same decision. That the field is a picklist is decided. What fills it, and who owns the list, are not.

First touch and last touch

Lead Source and Lead Source Detail are first touch: set at the first conversion and kept. The latest touch is recorded separately, in proposed most-recent UTM fields that are overwritten at each later conversion. They do not change Lead Source. A last-touch Lead Source is deferred. It is not designed or built.
